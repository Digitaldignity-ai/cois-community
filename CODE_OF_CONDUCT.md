# Community guidelines

This space is for constructive collaboration across experience levels and perspectives.

- Treat people respectfully. Critique ideas and evidence rather than attacking a person.
- Welcome questions and corrections. Do not demand that a newcomer already know the project history.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, impersonate others or publish private information.
- Keep discussion relevant; do not spam or misrepresent authorship, test results or affiliations.
- Respect a contributor's chosen public name and the limits of what they consent to share.

Maintainers may clarify expectations, moderate content, lock a conversation or restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Use GitHub's built-in reporting controls for abuse. For confidential vulnerability or exposure details, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); do not post them in a public conduct discussion.
